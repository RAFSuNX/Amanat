Visual Identity System
The mark of trust, in every form.

Amanat is a welfare system built on radical transparency. These guidelines define how the brand appears across all surfaces — print, digital, and document — so that every encounter communicates the same thing: this organisation handles what people give it with complete honesty.

01 — Brand Foundation

What Amanat means

Amanat is the Arabic word for trust — specifically the trust placed in a person when something precious is handed to them for safekeeping.

That meaning carries everything. Donors trust us with money that belongs to families they will never meet. Volunteers trust us with identities they have verified personally. Families trust us to deliver, month after month, without the amount shrinking on its way to them. The brand exists to make that trust visible and to keep it legible across every surface it touches.

Mission
Move money from those who have it to those who cannot sustain themselves, with a permanent, verifiable record of every taka.
Tagline
"The Hope for All of Us"
Used beneath the wordmark in formal contexts.
Principle
Everything Amanat does is publicly accountable. Nothing disappears.
02 — Wordmark

Logo & wordmark usage

The Amanat wordmark is set in Georgia — a humanist serif chosen for its warmth, legibility, and air of permanent record. The wordmark should always appear at a size where its serifs are readable. Never apply outline, shadow, or distortion.

03 — Colour

The palette

The palette draws from the deep greens of the Bengal landscape — paddy fields, river vegetation, forested hills. Every tone is a considered step in a single green family, from almost-black to near-white. There are no accent hues from outside this family; restraint is the mark of trust.

04 — Typography

Type system

Two typefaces. Georgia for display, narrative, and formal documents — its serifs carry the weight of a signed record. Geist (sans-serif) for all UI, labels, and data — the typeface used across the live website and admin system. Labels always uppercase, always tracked at 0.18–0.22em.

07 — Voice & Tone

How Amanat speaks

Amanat writes like a careful person who takes responsibility seriously — direct, warm without being familiar, and always specific. It never apologises for the gravity of what it does. It never oversells.

Accountable
Not bureaucratic

State exactly what happened and who is responsible for it. Avoid passive constructions that obscure ownership.

"Your donation of ৳5,000 has been confirmed and credited to the shared pool. Admin Hossain verified it on 27 September."
Warm
Not sentimental

Acknowledge what the person has done without leaning into emotion. The weight of the work does that for you.

"We urge you to keep an eye on the ledger until your donation is verified and added to the pool — so you know it arrived properly."
Plain
Not colloquial

Write for a first-time reader who has never encountered welfare systems. Avoid jargon. Define terms on first use.

"The distribution cycle is the monthly process by which Amanat calculates and delivers funds to each approved family."
Write this
  • "Your donation reaches us confirmed."
  • "This receipt can be verified independently."
  • "Every taka in and every taka out is on record."
  • "Rejected — document unclear. Please resubmit."
Not this
  • "Amazing! Your generous gift changes lives!"
  • "We're sorry, there was an issue..."
  • "100% of donations go to the poor."
  • "Please try again later."
08 — Document Family

Print & document standards

All formal Amanat documents share a common anatomy: a 5px solid green top bar, the wordmark at upper left, a receipt or reference number at upper right, a footer with contact details and an authorised signatory block, and a 3px solid bottom bar. This structure is the same whether the document is a donation receipt, a distribution certificate, or a correspondence letter.
